## Competing offers in market

| Angle | Common sender | Their pitch | Our counter |
|-------|--------------|-------------|-------------|
| Multi-year transformation program | Incumbent SIs | Scale, breadth, existing relationship | Value shipped every twelve weeks; a program is a sequence of cycles, not a multi-year commitment |
| Time-and-materials staffing | SIs, body shops | Bench capacity on demand | Future Works does not sell hourly capacity; a small pod of named senior experts orchestrating AI agents |
| AI pilot / proof of concept | AI vendors, consultants | Low-risk experiment | Pilots are the problem (74% see no measurable AI value, BCG 2024); Future Works moves AI into the P&L with a Finance-signed baseline |
