# ICP — Future Works

Source: `raw/FW ICP_ Ideal Customer Profile and Segments 2025+` (ICP and Positioning Lock v1.0, R11, updated 27 Oct 2026), plus Buying Triggers & Signals and Positioning (R11, 4 Sep 2026). Where they conflict, the 27 Oct ICP doc wins.

**One-line ICP:** large and mid-market companies running large-scale physical operations in the US and Canada, with a real P&L problem AI can move, an executive willing to sign for the outcome, and an operator-level champion inside the business. No startups.

## Target Profile

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Company type | Large and mid-market companies that make, move, power, grow, build or heal something physical. Roughly half enterprise, half large mid-market. Startups out of scope. |
| Company size | **Outbound = Core band only:** 501+ employees, revenue roughly $250M+ (sweet spot $1B–$20B, no upper ceiling). Employee count is the lead filter; revenue confirms scale. |
| Industries | Tier 1 (active outbound): Manufacturing and Industrials, Energy, Health and Life Sciences, Logistics and Transportation, Consumer Products |
| Geography | United States primary; Canada full scope (Calgary active hub). Non-US HQ with US operations qualifies at parent level (Philips, Sun Life pattern). Mexican ops of US/global parents in scope via parent rule. |
| Business profile | Complex supply chains, physical assets, regulated workflows, or an operations-heavy P&L |
| Tech estate | Established ERP, TMS, WMS, PLM or MES footprint; real legacy integration risk |
| Current state | Running one or more transformation programs with an incumbent SI; often "in pilot purgatory" on AI |
| Existing spend | Active or expiring engagement with a Tier 1/2 SI (Accenture, Wipro, TCS, Infosys, Deloitte, EY, Capgemini; IBM also named in triggers doc) |
| Decision-maker titles | CIO, CDO, CIDO, CTO (owning operations), Chief Digital and Transformation Officer, Global Head of IT and Process Excellence, Chief Supply Chain Officer, Chief AI Officer, Chief Innovation/Transformation Officer, COO |
| Signals to target | See signal priority table below |

### Size bands

| Band | Definition | Route |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Core | 501+ employees, revenue ~$250M+ | Outbound and inbound. Standard motion. |
| Edge | 201–500 employees, or revenue $100M–$250M | Inbound only. Never outbound. Enters only with a quantified value line, as a Future Works exception decision. |
| Out | Under 200 employees, or under $100M revenue | Never route. No exceptions. |

### Tier 1 industries (build outbound lists here only)

1. **Manufacturing and Industrials:** machinery, equipment makers, industrial automation, conglomerates. Use plain "manufacturing"; "advanced manufacturing" is a keyword, never the label.
2. **Energy:** transition, grid, renewables, power. **Exclude upstream oil and gas extraction.**
3. **Health and Life Sciences:** lead with medical devices, health technology, pharmaceutical manufacturing. De-emphasize providers and payers.
4. **Logistics and Transportation:** trucking, rail, freight, warehousing, 3PL.
5. **Consumer Products:** makers of goods, including food production and packaging. (Not Retail, which is watch tier.)

### Watch tier (inbound / opportunistic only; do not source outbound without checking first)

Construction and Infrastructure, High Tech (semiconductors, electronics, hardware), Aerospace and Defense (outbound only commercial aerospace; defense watchlist only), Agriculture and Food, Automotive, Commercial Real Estate (warm inbound only), Chemicals, Utilities, Retail.

## Signal Priority

Score an account higher when 2+ signals appear in a 90-day window. Most signals decay in 60–180 days; a six-month-old signal is cold.

| Signal | Source | Priority | Why it works |
|--------|--------|----------|-------------|
| New CIO, Chief AI Officer, CSCO, CDO or Chief Transformation Officer in seat (under 90 days; new-in-seat under 12 months is strong) | Press releases, LinkedIn appointments | 1 | New execs make vendor decisions in their first two quarters; "highest-value window" per triggers doc |
| Incumbent SI contract at renewal / visible slippage / change-order fatigue | Earnings calls, analyst days, procurement RFIs, news | 1 | Displacement window; Future Works positions as lower-risk than the current integrator |
| AI program failure, write-off, quiet reset, or initiative gone quiet 2+ quarters | 10-K, earnings calls, board decks, media | 1 | Direct match to the core problem (AI stuck in pilots) |
| RFP / procurement language shift to outcome-based fees, fixed-fee phases, named resources, sandbox-first, 12–16 week phases, Finance validation clauses | RFP portals, procurement activity | 1 | Buyer has already rejected time-and-materials; RFP shifts precede selection by 90–120 days |
| Board / CEO mandate on transformation or AI ROI; new CEO (12 months) or new CFO (6 months) | Proxy statements, board minutes, earnings calls | 2 | Opens the value-methodology conversation |
| Operational shock: supply chain disruption, recall, freight blow-out, S&OP failure, 2+ quarters of margin compression | News, earnings | 2 | Creates a named P&L number to move |
| Restructuring, spin-off, IPO prep, activist or PE pressure; post-M&A integration in a Tier 1 industry | News, filings | 2 | Operational narrative must survive diligence; integration workstreams are FW-shaped |
| Nearshoring / footprint reconfiguration into Mexico by a US or global parent | News, filings | 2 | Explicitly named as an in-scope wave |
| Executive posting about "pilot to production", agents in production, outcome-based pricing, SI cycle-time complaints, "12-week" delivery | LinkedIn | 2 | Category-aware buyer warming up |
| Layoffs paired with an efficiency mandate | News | 3 | Partner conversation usually follows 30–90 days later |
| Post-conference activity (Gartner SCM Symposium, ASCM Connect, CIO-tier summits) | LinkedIn, event lists | 3 | Active benchmarking |

Only contact prospects when a signal is present. Cold outreach without a signal performs significantly worse.

## Avoid List

| Exclusion | Reason |
|-----------|--------|
| Under 200 employees or under $100M revenue (incl. startups, Series A–C) | Out band; value math fails at cycle pricing |
| 201–500 employees or $100M–$250M revenue | Edge band: inbound only, never outbound |
| European-only accounts with no US or Canadian entity | Standing disqualifier; "most common routing error" |
| Mexico as HQ country | Only Mexican ops of US/global parents qualify |
| Metals and Mining; upstream Oil and Gas extraction | Capex cadence fights the 12-week motion |
| Paper and Packaging as a standalone lens | Sits inside Manufacturing and Consumer Products |
| Standalone Defense (outbound) | Watchlist only |
| Private Equity, funds, investment banks, asset managers (as outbound target) | Inbound only by standing rule |
| Emerging-tech categories (Quantum, Web3, AR/VR, blockchain, gene therapy) | Not a primary lens |
| Staff augmentation / body-shop / time-and-materials requests | Future Works does not sell hourly bench capacity |
| Pilot-for-pilot's-sake requests with no production path | Breaks the outcome-staked model |
| Marketing buyers (CMOs, marketing ops) | Not in the buyer set |
| Accounts unwilling to let the CFO's office validate the baseline | Breaks the guarantee mechanics |
| No named executive owner for AI/transformation; tooling-procurement language ("we need a platform") | Disqualifying signals per triggers doc |

## Decision-Maker Notes

Three-tier buying committee. Map every account before briefing. Codenames are internal only.

| Title | What they care about | Copy angle |
|-------|---------------------|------------|
| **Gamora-CIO** (Level 1, buyer of record): CIO, CDO, CIDO, operational CTO, Chief Digital and Transformation Officer, Global Head of IT and Process Excellence | AI stuck in pilot, SI workstream underperforming, board transformation narrative | Lower-risk than the current integrator: named experts, sandbox-first, fees staked on results, built on your stack (vendor-agnostic) |
| **Gamora-COO** (Level 1): COO, EVP Operations, SVP Global/Operations | Operational KPIs missed, SI mobilization too slow, margin pressure | 12-week cycle against operational KPIs; service credit safety net |
| **Gamora-SC** (Level 1, highest signal weight): Chief Supply Chain Officer, EVP/SVP Supply Chain | Margin, freight, OTIF, working capital, allocation logic | Margin, freight, inventory and working capital moved in a quarter, verified by Finance |
| **Gamora-AI** (Level 1, high and growing): Chief AI Officer, Head of AI, VP of AI, Head of Data and AI | 74% AI failure rate, pilot-to-production pressure, model trust | AI in production where it changes the P&L. Not another pilot. Agents in production, not demos |
| **Gamora-DX** (Level 1, front door not closer): Chief Innovation Officer, Chief Transformation Officer, Head/VP of Innovation | Innovation theatre fatigue, prove-it-fast, board-ready proof | Sub-12-week phases, named experts, board-ready outcomes. Cheapest front door; close usually moves to CIO or CSCO |
| **Star-Lord CEO** (Level 0, influencer who can block) | Risk transfer, board narrative | Future Works absorbs the program risk. One-page brief, not a deck |
| **Star-Lord CFO** (Level 0, influencer who can block) | CFO-validatable value methodology, audit-proof bonus mechanics | Baseline locked with your Finance team in week 2; a third of the fee contingent on value your team validates |
| **Procurement** (Level 0) | Unusual commercial model | Outcome-staked model is unusual but precedented (PHL-1 reference SOW). Expect 3–8 weeks of procurement education |
| **Nebula-Champion** (Level 2, make-or-break): Logic Owner, Head of Program, Senior Director of Operations, Director of IT, Director of Technology Innovation, SVP Operations Technology | Day-to-day delivery with the pod | Not a buyer; required for qualification. No champion = delivery cannot land |
| **Groot-Operator** (Level 3): planners, operations / business process / supply chain managers | Daily users of the agentic system | Change management matters; not an outbound target |

**Buyer demographics:** age 40–65 (Tiers 1–2), 32–55 (Tier 3); tenure 2–7 years typical; reports to CEO (CIO, COO, CSCO, CFO) or to CIO/COO (CAIO, CINO); US metros overrepresented: New York, Boston, Chicago, Atlanta, Dallas, San Francisco, Seattle; Calgary in Canada.

**Where each buyer looks first:** CIO via Gartner/Forrester and peer CIOs; CSCO via Gartner SCM, ASCM, CSCMP; CAIO via AI research and peer CAIO groups; COO via peer networks and ops summits; DX via innovation summits and podcasts; CFO via CFO peer networks and audit-committee briefings. Warm intros (board, peer exec) are the fastest route for most tiers.

## ICP Notes

- **Never use "asset-heavy" in copy.** It is an internal scoring concept only; in copy it makes good-fit companies rule themselves out.
- **Keep size framing internal.** Employee/revenue floors never appear in public copy.
- **Supply chain is a capability, not an industry.** The old "Industrial Supply Chain" cluster is retired.
- **Consumer Products ≠ Retail.** Makers (Tier 1) vs sellers (watch tier). Do not merge.
- **Routing intro must include:** trigger event, incumbent SI, named executive sponsor, operator champion.
- **Conflict:** Sep docs set the outbound floor at 500+ employees / ~$200M revenue; the 27 Oct ICP doc sets 501+ / ~$250M. Using the newer figure. See MEMORY Open Questions.
- **Watch-out from `analysis/`:** the Oil & Energy draft list (`US+CA-OilEnergy-AllSizes-AllTitles-NoExcl`) includes "all sizes" and oil companies; upstream oil and gas extraction is out of scope per this ICP. Filter before use.
