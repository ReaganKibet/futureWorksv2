## The Outcome Promise

**Big claim:** Value realized in twelve weeks: delivering in twelve weeks what incumbents quote at two years.
**Timeframe:** Twelve-week cycles; value verified by the client's Finance team at week 12.
**Guarantee / risk reversal:** Outcome-staked fee, validated by the client's Finance team. Each cycle fee is split into three equal tranches: (1) Mobilization at signature, (2) Build and Deploy around week 6, (3) Value Verification at week 12. Tranche 3 is conditional on Finance-validated value against the week-2 baseline:
- **Below target:** tranche 3 converts to a Service Credit on the next phase (90-day expiry).
- **At target:** full fee confirmed; tranche 3 invoices on financial sign-off.
- **Above target:** 15% Overdrive Bonus on every dollar of validated value above target (capped on the first 24-month annualized run rate).

"Risk reversal is the product. State it plainly and let the buyer do the math." Keep percentages and rate-card detail out of public surfaces.

**Retired commitments (never use):** "ROI or We Pay", "Free Until Value Pilot", "Twelve-Month Hypercare".

## Key Differentiators

1. **Velocity.** Twelve-week cycles with value shipped every cycle; a program is a sequence of cycles, not a multi-year commitment.
2. **Value-based, outcome-staked fees.** A third base, a third on delivery, a third on a validated value target. Service credit if they miss, bonus if they exceed. A time-and-materials pyramid structurally cannot do this.
3. **AI agents plus named experts.** A small pod of named senior experts, each orchestrating a fleet of high-accuracy AI agents on a proprietary, secure system. No labor pyramid, no swap-outs.

**Trust attributes to lead with everywhere:** Secure (enterprise-grade, compliant, sandbox-first) · Composable (vendor-agnostic, fits the existing estate) · Auditable (every outcome verified against a baseline the client signs).

**Seven commitments:** Named Senior Experts · Value-Based Fees · Sub-Twelve-Week Phases · Change-Order-Free Sprints · Transparent Resource Plan · Vendor-Agnostic Architecture · Auditable by Design.

## Positioning

**Against the market:** Against incumbent systems integrators (Accenture, Deloitte, Wipro, TCS, Infosys, EY, Capgemini, IBM) that bill time and materials, run multi-year programs, and stake nothing on the result. "We are not a systems integrator, and we are not an AI vendor. We are the engine that turns AI investment into P&L outcomes inside a quarter." Do not name competitors in copy.
**Who they are not for:** Startups and companies under 200 employees / $100M revenue; European-only companies; staff augmentation or hourly requests; pilots with no production path; marketing buyers; buyers unwilling to let Finance validate the baseline. Qualification needs all three: a real P&L problem AI can move, an executive willing to sign for outcomes, an operator-level champion.

**What each buyer must hear (client-supplied):**

| Buyer | The one message |
|-------|----------------|
| CIO or CTO | Lower-risk than the current integrator. Named experts, sandbox-first, fees staked on results, built on your stack. |
| Chief Supply Chain Officer | Margin, freight, inventory and working capital moved in a quarter, verified by Finance. |
| Chief AI Officer | AI in production where it changes the P&L. Not another pilot. |
| CFO or CEO | Future Works absorbs the program risk. A third of the fee is contingent on value your team validates. |

**Why now:** AI has crossed from experiment to operating imperative; the gap between companies with AI in production and those still piloting is measurable in P&L points. Buyers who wait carry the transformation risk themselves.
