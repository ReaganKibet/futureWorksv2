## What They Do

Future Works is "the AI-native transformation engine". It rebuilds the operations that move the P&L (manufacturing, energy, health and life sciences, logistics, and supply chain) in twelve-week cycles, run by named senior experts who each orchestrate a fleet of AI agents on a proprietary, secure stack. A third of every cycle fee is staked on a value target the client's Finance team validates: service credit if they miss, bonus if they exceed. The differentiator is pace and risk: they deliver in twelve weeks what incumbent systems integrators quote at two years, and they carry program risk instead of billing time and materials.

**Category line:** AI transformation with skin in the game.
**Subhead:** Outcome-based AI transformation, delivered in twelve-week cycles.
**Internal descriptors (not public leads):** AI Native Operating Partner; Service as Software.

**Mission:** Move AI out of pilots and into the parts of the business that change the P&L, on a twelve-week clock, with a third of every fee staked on a value target the client's Finance team validates.

**Vision:** Be the AI-native transformation engine that operators pick over the incumbent integrators, because they deliver in twelve weeks what those integrators quote at two years, and absorb the program risk buyers currently carry.

**Values:** Outcome-staked · Delivering Joy ("promise 95, deliver 110") · Innovative · Selfless · Candid · Free · Excellent.
